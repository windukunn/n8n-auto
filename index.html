n8n Auto

Automates posting videos to TikTok on behalf of authorized creators, using n8n workflow automation and TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

n8n Auto is a self-hosted n8n workflow that connects to a creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official OAuth login, then publishes video content on their behalf. No credentials are ever handled directly — everything goes through TikTok's APIs.

  1. Connect your TikTok account. The creator clicks a login link generated by the workflow and is redirected to TikTok's own authorization screen.
  2. Grant permission. TikTok asks the creator to approve access to their basic profile and posting permissions. Nothing is shared until this step is confirmed by the creator on TikTok's own page.
  3. Workflow picks up content. The n8n workflow reads a video queued for publishing (from a folder, spreadsheet, or connected storage) along with its caption and settings.
  4. Video is uploaded via the Content Posting API. The workflow calls TikTok's video.upload / video.publish endpoints to send the file and publish it to the authorized account.
  5. Status is confirmed. The workflow checks video.list to confirm the post went live and logs the result back to the creator.

Access can be revoked by the creator at any time from their TikTok account settings, which immediately stops the workflow from posting on their behalf.

What it does

  • Scheduled & batch posting — queue multiple videos and let the workflow publish them automatically.
  • Authorized, per-creator access — every account connects through its own TikTok OAuth login; no shared or scraped credentials.
  • Publish confirmation — each upload is verified against the creator's post list after publishing.
  • Basic profile & stats lookup — used to show the creator which account is connected and confirm the post was attributed correctly.

FAQ

Who can use this? Only creators who explicitly authorize their own TikTok account through TikTok's login screen. No account is accessed without that consent.

Does this post without my approval? No — every video published comes from content the creator has queued into their own workflow beforehand.

Can I disconnect? Yes, at any time from TikTok's account settings under "Manage apps and devices."

Is this affiliated with TikTok? No. n8n Auto is an independent automation workflow built on n8n and TikTok's public developer APIs.
